# Battery-X1 Portable Power Station — Product Specification

Battery-X1 is a portable lithium power station designed for outdoor use and emergency backup. The product is manufactured by NovaCell Energy and distributed in the EU and North America.

## 1. Electrical Specification

| Parameter | Value | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rated capacity | 1024 | Wh |
| Output power | 600 | W |
| Peak power | 1200 | W |
| Cell chemistry | LiFePO4 | - |
| Cycle life | 3000 | cycles |
| Charge time (AC) | 1.8 | hours |
| Input voltage | 100-240 | V |

Battery-X1 uses LiFePO4 cells, which provide higher thermal stability than conventional NMC cells. The rated cycle life is 3000 cycles to 80% of original capacity.

## 2. Safety and Certification

Battery-X1 requires UN38.3 certification for air transport. The product also carries CE certification for the European market and FCC certification for the United States market. The built-in battery management system (BMS) provides over-voltage, over-current and short-circuit protection.

The operating temperature range is -10°C to 40°C. Charging is disabled below 0°C to prevent lithium plating. The product must not be exposed to water or stored in a vehicle cabin above 60°C.

## 3. Charging and Output

Battery-X1 supports three charging methods: AC wall charging, solar charging and car charging. Solar input accepts 12-30V and a maximum of 200W. The AC output provides a pure sine wave with total harmonic distortion below 3%.

The USB-C port supports 100W Power Delivery. The 12V DC port is limited to 120W. When the total load exceeds 600W, the inverter shuts down within 10 milliseconds and the overload indicator turns red.

## 4. Warranty and Service

NovaCell Energy provides a 24-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage caused by water ingress, unauthorized disassembly or operation outside the specified temperature range.

Customers in the EU have a 14-day statutory right of withdrawal. Warranty claims must include the serial number and proof of purchase.

## 5. Logistics and Customs

Each carton contains one Battery-X1 unit and a 1.5-meter AC cable. The shipping weight is 13.5 kg. The HS code for Battery-X1 is 8507.60. Lithium batteries are classified as Class 9 dangerous goods for air freight.

Exporters must provide the UN38.3 test summary and the MSDS document for customs clearance. The commercial invoice must state the declared value, the HS code and the country of origin.
